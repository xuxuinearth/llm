## 示例

以
$$
y = \text{ReLU}(W x)
$$
其中
$$
W = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.2 \\ 0.3 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix}, \quad
x = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}, \quad
y = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$
$$
\mathcal{L} = -\left[ y \log(p) + (1-y) \log(1-p) \right]
$$
为例进行说明。

根据
$$
y = \text{ReLU}(W x), \quad
W = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.2 \\ 0.3 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix}, \quad
x = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}
$$
得出
$$
p = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 \\ 1.1 \end{bmatrix}
$$
对 $\mathbf{W}$ 求导，因为 
$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial \mathbf{W}} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial p} \cdot \frac{\partial p}{\partial \mathbf{W}} \qquad (1)
$$ 
$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial p} = -\frac{y}{p} + \frac{1-y}{1-p} 
\qquad (2)
$$ 
$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial p} = \frac{p - y}{p(1-p)} 
\qquad (3)
$$ 
$$\frac{\partial p}{\partial \mathbf{W}} = 
\begin{cases} 
x, & wx > 0 \\ 
0, & wx < 0 
\end{cases} \tag{4}$$
所以：
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \mathbf{W}}=\begin{bmatrix}
 \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} W_{11} }  & \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} W_{12} }\\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} W_{21} } & \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} W_{22} }
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
 \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{1} }\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{1}}{\mathrm{d} W_{11} }  & \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{1} }\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{1}}{\mathrm{d} W_{12} }\\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{1}} \frac{\mathrm{d} p_{1}}{\mathrm{d} W_{21} }& \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{1}}\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{1}}{\mathrm{d} W_{22}}
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
 \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{2} }\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{2}}{\mathrm{d} W_{11} }  & \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{2} }\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{2}}{\mathrm{d} W_{12} }\\
  \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{2}} \frac{\mathrm{d} p_{2}}{\mathrm{d} W_{21} }& \frac{\mathrm{d} L}{\mathrm{d} p_{2}}\frac{\mathrm{d} p_{2}}{\mathrm{d} W_{22}}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
 -2  & -4 \\
  0 & 0
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
 0  & 0 \\
 -\frac{10}{11}  &-\frac{20}{11}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
-2  & -4\\
  -\frac{10}{11}  &-\frac{20}{11}
\end{bmatrix}$$
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \mathbf{W}} = [-0.5,1]^T[1,2]$$

# AdaGrad中w更新示例
### 更新公式

累积平方梯度：

$$
G_t = G_{t-1} + \Delta l_{i}^2 ( w_{t})
$$

参数更新：

$$
W_{\text{new}} = W - \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{G_t} + \epsilon} \cdot \Delta l_{i}( w_{t})
$$

### 计算过程

初始状态：$v_0 = 0$

第 1 步累积：

$$
G_t = \Delta l_{i}^2( w_{t}) = \begin{bmatrix} (-2)^2 & (-4)^2 \\ \left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^2 & \left(\frac{20}{11}\right)^2 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 16 \\ \frac{100}{121} & \frac{400}{121} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 16 \\ 0.8264 & 3.3058 \end{bmatrix}
$$

逐元素更新（$\epsilon = 0$）：

**$W_{11}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{4}} \times (-2) = \frac{0.1}{2} \times (-2) = -0.1
$$

$$
W_{11}^{\text{new}} = 0.1 - (-0.1) = 0.2
$$

**$W_{12}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{16}} \times (-4) = \frac{0.1}{4} \times (-4) = -0.1
$$

$$
W_{12}^{\text{new}} = 0.2 - (-0.1) = 0.3
$$

**$W_{21}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{100/121}} \times \frac{10}{11}
= \frac{0.1}{10/11} \times \frac{10}{11} = 0.1
$$

$$
W_{21}^{\text{new}} = 0.3 - 0.1 = 0.2
$$

**$W_{22}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{400/121}} \times \frac{20}{11}
= \frac{0.1}{20/11} \times \frac{20}{11} = 0.1
$$

$$
W_{22}^{\text{new}} = 0.4 - 0.1 = 0.3
$$

### 结果

$$
\boxed{
W_{\text{AdaGrad}} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 & 0.3 \\ 0.2 & 0.3 \end{bmatrix}
}
$$


# RMSProp中w更新示例
### 更新公式

指数移动平均平方梯度：

$$
v_t = \beta v_{t-1} + (1-\beta) \Delta l_{i}^2( w_{t})
$$

参数更新：

$$
W_{\text{new}} = W - \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{v_t} + \epsilon} \cdot \Delta l_{i}( w_{t})
$$

### 计算过程

初始状态：$v_0 = 0$，$\beta = 0.5$

第 1 步计算：

$$
v = 0.5 \times 0 + 0.5 \times \Delta l_{i}^2( w_{t})
= 0.5 \times \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 16 \\ \frac{100}{121} & \frac{400}{121} \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 8 \\ \frac{50}{121} & \frac{200}{121} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 8 \\ 0.4132 & 1.6529 \end{bmatrix}
$$

逐元素更新（$\epsilon = 0$）：

**$W_{11}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{2}} \times (-2) \approx -0.1414
$$

$$
W_{11}^{\text{new}} = 0.1 - (-0.1414) = 0.2414
$$

**$W_{12}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{8}} \times (-4) \approx -0.1414
$$

$$
W_{12}^{\text{new}} = 0.2 - (-0.1414) = 0.3414
$$

**$W_{21}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{50/121}} \times \frac{10}{11}
= \frac{0.1}{5\sqrt{2}/11} \times \frac{10}{11}
= \frac{0.1}{5\sqrt{2}} \times 10 \approx 0.1414
$$

$$
W_{21}^{\text{new}} = 0.3 - 0.1414 = 0.1586
$$

**$W_{22}$：**

$$
\Delta = \frac{0.1}{\sqrt{200/121}} \times \frac{20}{11}
= \frac{0.1}{10\sqrt{2}/11} \times \frac{20}{11}
= \frac{0.1}{10\sqrt{2}} \times 20 \approx 0.1414
$$

$$
W_{22}^{\text{new}} = 0.4 - 0.1414 = 0.2586
$$

# Adam中w更新示例
### 更新公式

**第一步：更新一阶矩估计（动量）**

$$
m_t = \beta_1 m_{t-1} + (1 - \beta_1) \Delta l_{i}( w_{t})
$$

**第二步：更新二阶矩估计（自适应学习率）**

$$
v_t = \beta_2 v_{t-1} + (1 - \beta_2) \Delta l_{i}^2( w_{t})
$$

**第三步：偏差校正**

$$
\hat{m}_t = \frac{m_t}{1 - \beta_1^t}
$$

$$
\hat{v}_t = \frac{v_t}{1 - \beta_2^t}
$$

**第四步：参数更新**

$$
W_{\text{new}} = W - \eta \cdot \frac{\hat{m}_t}{\sqrt{\hat{v}_t} + \epsilon}
$$

### 计算过程



**第一步：更新一阶矩 $m$**

$$
m_1 = \beta_1 m_0 + (1 - \beta_1) \Delta l_{i}( w_{t})
= 0.5 \times 0 + 0.5 \times \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -4 \\ \frac{10}{11} & \frac{20}{11} \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ \frac{5}{11} & \frac{10}{11} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ 0.4545 & 0.9091 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**第二步：更新二阶矩 $v$（逐元素平方）**

$$
v_1 = \beta_2 v_0 + (1 - \beta_2) \Delta l_{i}( w_{t})
= 0.5 \times 0 + 0.5 \times \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 16 \\ \frac{100}{121} & \frac{400}{121} \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 8 \\ \frac{50}{121} & \frac{200}{121} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 8 \\ 0.4132 & 1.6529 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**第三步：偏差校正**

$$
\hat{m}_1 = \frac{m_1}{1 - \beta_1^1} = \frac{m_1}{1 - 0.5} = 2 \times m_1
= \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -4 \\ \frac{10}{11} & \frac{20}{11} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -4 \\ 0.9091 & 1.8182 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\hat{v}_1 = \frac{v_1}{1 - \beta_2^1} = \frac{v_1}{1 - 0.5} = 2 \times v_1
= \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 16 \\ \frac{100}{121} & \frac{400}{121} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 16 \\ 0.8264 & 3.3058 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**第四步：参数更新**

$$
W_{\text{Adam}} = W - \eta \cdot \frac{\hat{m}_1}{\sqrt{\hat{v}_1} + \epsilon}
$$

逐元素计算（$\epsilon = 0$）：

**$W_{11}$：**

$$
\Delta = 0.1 \times \frac{-2}{\sqrt{4}} = 0.1 \times \frac{-2}{2} = -0.1
$$

$$
W_{11}^{\text{new}} = 0.1 - (-0.1) = 0.2
$$

**$W_{12}$：**

$$
\Delta = 0.1 \times \frac{-4}{\sqrt{16}} = 0.1 \times \frac{-4}{4} = -0.1
$$

$$
W_{12}^{\text{new}} = 0.2 - (-0.1) = 0.3
$$

**$W_{21}$：**

$$
\Delta = 0.1 \times \frac{10/11}{\sqrt{100/121}}
= 0.1 \times \frac{10/11}{10/11} = 0.1
$$

$$
W_{21}^{\text{new}} = 0.3 - 0.1 = 0.2
$$

**$W_{22}$：**

$$
\Delta = 0.1 \times \frac{20/11}{\sqrt{400/121}}
= 0.1 \times \frac{20/11}{20/11} = 0.1
$$

$$
W_{22}^{\text{new}} = 0.4 - 0.1 = 0.3
$$

### 结果

$$
\boxed{
W_{\text{Adam}} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 & 0.3 \\ 0.2 & 0.3 \end{bmatrix}
}
$$

# Muon 优化器更新示例

### 更新公式

**第一步：计算动量**

$$
M_t = \beta_1 M_{t-1} + (1 - \beta_1) G_t
$$

其中 $G_t = \nabla_W \mathcal{L}$ 为当前梯度。

**第二步：归一化**

$$
X_0 = \frac{M_t}{\|M_t\|_F}
$$

**第三步：Newton-Schulz 正交化迭代**

5 次多项式迭代（重复 5 次）：

$$
X_{k+1} = a X_k + b (X_k X_k^T) X_k + c (X_k X_k^T)^2 X_k
$$

其中：
- $a = 3.4445$
- $b = -4.7750$
- $c = 2.0315$

**第四步：参数更新**

$$
Q = X_5 \cdot \frac{M_t}{\|M_t\|_F}
$$

$$
W_{\text{new}} = W - \eta \cdot Q
$$

---

### 计算过程

**初始状态：** $M_0 = 0$，$\beta_1 = 0.5$

**第一步：动量累积**

$$
M_t = \beta_1 M_{t-1} + (1 - \beta_1) G_t
= 0.5 \times 0 + 0.5 \times \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -4 \\ \frac{10}{11} & \frac{20}{11} \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ \frac{5}{11} & \frac{10}{11} \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ 0.4545 & 0.9091 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**第二步：归一化**

计算 Frobenius 范数：

$$
\|M_t\|_F = \sqrt{(-1)^2 + (-2)^2 + \left(\frac{5}{11}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^2}
= \sqrt{1 + 4 + \frac{25}{121} + \frac{100}{121}}
= \sqrt{5 + \frac{125}{121}}
= \sqrt{\frac{605 + 125}{121}}
= \sqrt{\frac{730}{121}}
= \frac{\sqrt{730}}{11}
\approx \frac{27.0185}{11}
\approx 2.4562
$$

归一化：

$$
X_0 = \frac{M_t}{\|M_t\|_F}
= \frac{1}{2.4562} \begin{bmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ 0.4545 & 0.9091 \end{bmatrix}
\approx \begin{bmatrix} -0.4071 & -0.8143 \\ 0.1851 & 0.3701 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**第三步：Newton-Schulz 正交化迭代**

**3.1 第 1 次迭代（$k=0 \rightarrow 1$）**

计算 $A = X_0 X_0^T$：

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} -0.4071 & -0.8143 \\ 0.1851 & 0.3701 \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} -0.4071 & 0.1851 \\ -0.8143 & 0.3701 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 0.8288 & -0.3768 \\ -0.3768 & 0.1713 \end{bmatrix}
$$

计算 $A^2 = A \cdot A$：

$$
A^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.8288 & -0.3768 \\ -0.3768 & 0.1713 \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} 0.8288 & -0.3768 \\ -0.3768 & 0.1713 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 0.8289 & -0.3768 \\ -0.3768 & 0.1713 \end{bmatrix}
\approx A
$$

应用 5 次多项式迭代：

$$
X_1 = a X_0 + b A X_0 + c A^2 X_0
$$

由于 $A X_0 = X_0$ 且 $A^2 X_0 = X_0$：

$$
X_1 = (a + b + c) X_0 = (3.4445 - 4.7750 + 2.0315) X_0 = 0.7010 X_0
$$

$$
X_1 \approx 0.7010 \times \begin{bmatrix} -0.4071 & -0.8143 \\ 0.1851 & 0.3701 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} -0.2854 & -0.5708 \\ 0.1298 & 0.2595 \end{bmatrix}
$$

**3.2 第 2 次迭代（$k=1 \rightarrow 2$）**

计算 $A = X_1 X_1^T$：

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} -0.2854 & -0.5708 \\ 0.1298 & 0.2595 \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} -0.2854 & 0.1298 \\ -0.5708 & 0.2595 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 0.4076 & -0.1853 \\ -0.1853 & 0.0842 \end{bmatrix}
$$

计算 $A^2$：

$$
A^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4076 & -0.1853 \\ -0.1853 & 0.0842 \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} 0.4076 & -0.1853 \\ -0.1853 & 0.0842 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} 0.2004 & -0.0911 \\ -0.0911 & 0.0414 \end{bmatrix}
$$

计算 $A X_1$：

$$
A X_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4076 & -0.1853 \\ -0.1853 & 0.0842 \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} -0.2854 & -0.5708 \\ 0.1298 & 0.2595 \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} -0.1404 & -0.2808 \\ 0.0638 & 0.1276 \end{bmatrix}
$$

计算 $A^2 X_1$：

$$
A^2 X_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2004 & -0.0911 \\ -0.0911 & 0.0414 \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} -0.2854 & -0.5708 \\ 0.1298 & 0.2595 \end{bmatrix}

$$
X_2 = a X_1 + b A X_1 + c A^2 X_1
$$

$$
