## 示例

以 $y = \text{ReLU}(W x)$,其中 $W = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.2 \\ 0.3 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix}$ ,

$x = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix}$,
$y = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$

$$
\mathcal{L} = -\left[ y \log(p) + (1-y) \log(1-p) \right]
$$
为例进行说明。

根据
$$
y = \text{ReLU}(W x), \quad
W = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.2 \\ 0.3 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix}, \quad
x = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix}
$$
得出
$$
p = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 \\ 1.1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial \mathbf{W}} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial p} \cdot \frac{\partial p}{\partial \mathbf{W}} \qquad (1)
$$ 
$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial p} = -\frac{y}{p} + \frac{1-y}{1-p} 
\qquad (2)
$$ 
$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial p} = \frac{p - y}{p(1-p)} 
\qquad (3)
$$ 
$$\frac{\partial p}{\partial \mathbf{W}} = 
\begin{cases} 
x, & wx > 0 \\ 
0, & wx < 0 
\end{cases} \tag{3}$$
\\
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \mathbf{W}} = [-0.5,1]^T[1,2]$$
